CONSTITUTION AND RULES OF BETTER LIFE COMMUNITY BASED ORGANIZATION (CBO)
1. NAME, PHYSICAL ADDRESS
The name of the organization shall be Better Life CBO (Community Based Organization),
a non-political, non-sectarian, and voluntary community organization committed to
enhancing the social and economic wellbeing of its members and the wider
community.
The organization shall be domiciled in Kahawa West, Roysambu Subcounty, Nairobi City
County.
Email: cbobetterlife@gmail.com
2. MOTTO, VISION, MISSION, AND CORE VALUES
Motto: Empowering Lives for a Better Tomorrow.
Vision: A transformed and self-reliant community living a better life.
Mission: To empower individuals and families through community-driven initiatives that
enhance social welfare, education, environmental sustainability, and economic
growth.
Core Values:
1. Integrity and Transparency
2. Inclusivity and Equality
3. Accountability and Teamwork
4. Empowerment and Self-Reliance
5. Community Service and Sustainability

3. OBJECTIVES
The objectives of Better Life CBO shall be to:
1. Promote community empowerment through education, health, and economic
programs.
2. Support vulnerable groups including youth, women, the elderly, and persons with
disabilities.
3. Promote environmental conservation, sanitation, and sustainable living practices.
4. Facilitate skills training, entrepreneurship, and financial empowerment
opportunities.
5. Collaborate with government agencies, NGOs, and private entities in
implementing community development projects.
6. Foster unity, peace, and social responsibility within the community.
7. Mobilize resources to improve livelihoods through income-generating activities.
4. ACTIVITIES OF BETTER LIFE CBO
The organization shall undertake the following activities to achieve its objectives:
1. Economic Empowerment-support small businesses, capacity building, and
cooperative initiatives.
2. Education and Training – provide scholarships, mentorship, and skills training for
youth and women.
3. Health and Sanitation – organize community health drives, awareness
campaigns, and hygiene projects.

4. Environmental Conservation – initiate tree planting, waste management, and
clean-up campaigns.
5. Social Cohesion – facilitate youth mentorship, family strengthening, and peace-
building programs.
5. MEMBERSHIP
Membership shall be open to all persons aged 18 years and above residing within the
area of operation who subscribe to the mission and vision of Better Life CBO.
a) Registration:
• New members shall pay a non-refundable registration fee of Ksh 5,000.
• Each member shall make regular contributions as determined by the general
meeting to achieve the group’s activities.
• Every member shall complete a membership form and provide accurate contact
information.
b) Rights of Members:
• To attend and participate in all general meetings.
• To vote and be voted into any elective position.
• To inspect and receive information regarding the organization’s activities and
finances.
• To benefit from the programs and projects of the organization.
c) Duties of Members:

• To adhere to this Constitution and promote the mission of the organization.
• To make timely financial contributions when called upon.
• To participate actively in meetings and CBO activities.
• To respect the leadership and decisions made collectively.
d) Termination of Membership:
Membership shall cease upon:
• Written resignation addressed to the Secretary.
• Failure to participate in CBO’s activities.
• Gross misconduct, fraud, or actions detrimental to the CBO’s reputation.
A member whose membership is terminated may appeal in writing to the Executive
Committee within 30 days.
6. GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE
The governance of Better Life CBO shall consist of:
a) The General Assembly – composed of all registered members, as the supreme
decision-making body.
b) The Executive Committee – responsible for implementation of decisions and
management of daily operations.
c) Subcommittees – established as necessary to address specific areas such as finance,
programs, or welfare.
7. OFFICE BEARERS AND THEIR DUTIES

The office bearers of Better Life CBO shall be:
1. Chairperson
2. Vice Chairperson
3. Secretary
4. Vice Secretary
5. Treasurer
Duties:
Chairperson:
- Presides over all meetings.
- Provides strategic leadership and ensures implementation of resolutions.
- Represents the CBO in official matters.
Vice Chairperson:
- Assists and deputizes the Chairperson.
- Undertakes delegated responsibilities as needed.
Secretary:
- Keeps accurate records of meetings and correspondence.
- Issues notices of meetings and maintains official documents.
- Prepares periodic and annual reports.
Vice Secretary:
- Assists the Secretary and acts in their absence.
Treasurer:
Page 6 of 8
- Receives and accounts for all funds.
- Prepares and presents quarterly financial reports.
- Maintains proper accounting records and ensures transparency.
8. MEETINGS
a) Regular Meetings: Held quarterly to review progress and discuss ongoing activities.
b) Annual General Meeting (AGM): Convened once each year to present reports, elect
officials, and approve plans.
c) Special Meetings: May be called by the Chairperson or upon written request by at
least one-third of members.
Quorum:
- For general meetings: one-third of registered members.
- For Executive Committee meetings: two-thirds of committee members.
Procedure:
- The Chairperson shall preside; in their absence, the Vice Chairperson or Secretary shall
do so.
- Decisions shall be made by simple majority vote, except where otherwise stated.
9. FINANCES AND ACCOUNTABILITY
a) Sources of Funds:
- Members’ registration fees and contributions.
- Grants, donations, and fundraising activities.
- Income from lawful investments or projects.
b) Management of Funds:
Page 7 of 8
- The organization shall maintain a bank account in its name, operated by three
signatories: Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer (any two to sign).
- All expenditures shall be authorized and properly documented.
c) Financial Records and Auditing:
- The Treasurer shall maintain accurate records of income and expenditure.
- An annual financial statement shall be prepared and presented at the AGM.
- Accounts shall be audited at least once per year by an appointed auditor.
10. DISCIPLINE AND CONFLICT RESOLUTION
Members and officials shall conduct themselves with integrity and respect. Any breach
shall attract disciplinary action which may include warning, suspension, or expulsion.
A Disciplinary Committee shall handle internal disputes. Where unresolved, matters may
be referred to the relevant Subcounty Social Development Office or alternative dispute
resolution mechanisms.
11. ELECTIONS AND TENURE
• Elections shall be held every two (2) years during the AGM.
• All positions shall be filled through free and fair secret ballot or through consensus.
• Only members in good standing are eligible for election.
• In case of resignation, death, or removal, the Executive Committee may appoint
a replacement until the next election.
12. AMENDMENT OF THE CONSTITUTION

This Constitution may be amended at a duly convened general meeting by a two-thirds
majority vote of the members present. Any amendment shall be submitted to the
Ministry of Labour and Social Protection for approval and registration.
13. INDEMNITY
All office bearers shall be indemnified by the organization against any losses or liabilities
incurred in the lawful execution of their duties. However, they shall be personally liable
for any losses resulting from willful negligence, fraud, or unauthorized actions.
14. DISSOLUTION
The organization may be dissolved by a resolution passed by at least two-thirds of
registered members during a general meeting. Upon dissolution, all assets shall be used
to settle liabilities, and any remaining property shall be donated to another organization
with similar objectives, subject to approval by the Ministry of Labour and Social
Protection.
